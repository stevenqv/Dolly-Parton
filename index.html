About Dolly

Dolly Parton is an award-winning singer, songwriter, actress, author, and philanthropist whose career has spanned more than six decades. She is widely recognized for her contributions to country music and for her positive impact on communities around the world.

Beyond her music, Dolly is known for her generosity and lifelong commitment to education, literacy, and helping others. Her career demonstrates how creativity, business, and service can work together to make a lasting impact.

Interesting Facts

  • Born in Sevier County, Tennessee.
  • Founded the Imagination Library in 1995.
  • Has written thousands of songs throughout her career.
  • Received numerous Grammy Awards and lifetime achievement honors.

Philanthropy

The Imagination Library

Dolly Parton's Imagination Library has provided hundreds of millions of free books to children around the world, encouraging literacy and a lifelong love of reading.
